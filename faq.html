How much does mobile detailing cost?

Pricing depends on vehicle size, current condition, service level, and location. Basic details may start around $150–$200, while premium services may be higher.

Do you service condos and apartments?

Yes, as long as the property allows mobile detailing and has a suitable work area.

Do you offer monthly maintenance?

Yes. Monthly maintenance is available for clients who want their vehicle kept consistently clean.

What areas do you service?

Common areas include Kapolei, Ewa Beach, Makakilo, Ko Olina, Honolulu, Kakaako, Waikiki, Pearl City, and nearby Oahu areas by request.
